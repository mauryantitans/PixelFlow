# Code of Conduct

PixelFlow adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text there applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in
issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space, and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## In short

We want a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behaviour**

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and levels of knowledge.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer:

- Send a private message to the maintainer through their GitHub profile,
  [@Mauryantitans](https://github.com/Mauryantitans), or
- if the problem is in a specific comment, use GitHub's **Report content** option on it (the `...`
  menu), which can go to the repository maintainers.

Please include links to the comments, issues or pull requests involved and a short description of
what happened. Reports are handled confidentially, and you won't face retaliation for making one in
good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response that fits what happened, following the
Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, such as a period of no
   interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban**: a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban**: a permanent ban from the project's community spaces.

Comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code of conduct may be edited,
hidden or removed.
